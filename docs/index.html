🌾 Digital Farming Companion

Empowering Cane Growers with Smart Kissan

A fast, reliable, offline-first mobile application designed to help sugarcane growers track weighment slips, harvest batches, and safeguard their farming records.

⚡

Quick Ticket & Batch Entry

Easily record cane ticket numbers, vehicle weights, deductions, and gross totals with swift numeric and voice input assistance.

☁️

Secure Google Drive Backup

Your agricultural data is backed up safely to your personal Google Drive account. Only app-created backup files are accessed.

🔒

Offline-First & Private

Works seamlessly without an internet connection in remote fields. Your data remains stored on your own device and private drive.

📊

Season Analysis & Reports

Gain valuable insights into harvest yield, average supply weights, and crushing season timelines across multiple field plots.
