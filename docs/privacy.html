Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 11, 2026 | Effective Immediately

1. Introduction

Welcome to Smart Kissan ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Aryan Tomar. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and confidentiality of farmers and agricultural users who use our mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, stored, and utilized when you use the Smart Kissan app, including our integration with Google OAuth and Google Drive APIs.

2. Information We Collect

Smart Kissan follows a local-first philosophy. We do not operate external tracking servers or harvest personal details. The categories of information processed include:

  • Farm & Ticket Data: Cane ticket numbers, vehicle gross/tare weights, harvest dates, and crop seasons entered manually or via voice assistance. This information is stored locally on your device.
  • Google Account Information (Optional): When you opt-in to the Cloud Backup feature, we receive your basic Google account profile information (email address and account identifier) via Google Sign-In solely to link and display your connected backup account.

3. Google API Services & Drive Access

To prevent data loss and allow seamless phone upgrades, Smart Kissan offers an optional Cloud Backup integration powered by the Google Drive API.

Scope Requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file (Per-file access). Smart Kissan ONLY accesses files and folders that the app itself creates (e.g., SmartKissan_Backups). Smart Kissan has NO access to your personal photos, documents, or other files stored on your Google Drive.

When you enable Cloud Backup:

  • The app generates an export file of your local ticket records and uploads it directly to your personal Google Drive storage.
  • When you choose to restore data, the app downloads and extracts your previously saved backup file.
  • Your Google OAuth tokens are stored securely on your device using encrypted storage (Android Keystore / SecureStore) and are never transmitted to third parties.

4. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Smart Kissan adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We do not transfer or share your Google user data with any third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing platforms.
  • We do not use your Google Drive files or Google account data to train artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
  • Human access to your data is strictly prohibited; all synchronization happens automatically and directly between your device and Google's official Drive API endpoints.

5. Data Storage & Security

We implement industry-standard safeguards to protect your records:

  • All local data is stored within the app's sandboxed SQLite database on your device.
  • Cloud communications with Google Drive use industry-standard HTTPS encryption (TLS 1.3).
  • We do not maintain centralized cloud databases containing user records; you retain full control and ownership of your data on your personal Google Drive.

6. User Control & Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data at all times:

  • Disconnect Google Drive: You can disconnect your Google account anytime via the in-app Backup settings screen. This will immediately purge stored tokens from your device.
  • Delete Cloud Backups: You can delete backup archives directly from your Google Drive folder or inside the app.
  • Revoke Permissions: You can revoke Smart Kissan's access at any time through your Google Account Security Dashboard.
